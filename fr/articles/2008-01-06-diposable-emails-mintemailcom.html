Diposable emails @ Mintemail.com

6 janvier 2008
Internet & tech

I didn't know about this site before today.

Mintemail provides you freely with disposable (temporary) email addresses.

It just super simple to use

  1. You go to the website (http://www.mintemail.com)
  2. Your new temporary email address has been already saved to your clipboard (no need to press Ctrl+C). It also displayed in the url (example: http://www.mintemail.com/?email=ashley9019) and on the webpage (example: ashley9019@mintemail.uni.cc)
  3. As soon as you're receiving emails, they displayed on the same page.

Nice ...
