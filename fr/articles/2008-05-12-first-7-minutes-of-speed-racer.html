First 7 minutes of Speed Racer ...

12 mai 2008
Cinéma

Until now, I was not convinced by the next Wachowski brothers flick: Speed Racer. After seeing the first 7 minutes, I'm still not convinced at all. As for the movies critics, only a few of them really like the movie, as for example TIME Magazine "More than the story of the Racer family, Speed Racer is the visual autobiography of the Wachowskis and their pit crew of computer-nerd Einsteins, using the tools of their trade to transform the movie medium."
