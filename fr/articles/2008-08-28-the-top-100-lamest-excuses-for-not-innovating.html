Uncategorized

Patrice, Glad you liked my post. One way to get value from this list is to turn each of the excuses into an open-ended question starting with the words "How can I" or "How can we?" Then, after you have your new question, brainstorm it, alone or with your team. So, if the excuse is, "No one, except me, really cares about innovation" you might turn it into a question like "How can I identify the people here who really care about innovation?" or "How can I help others here care about innovation as much as me?" or "How can I influence the senior team to help get the word out about the importance of innovation?" etc. This is a technique we teach called "Lead Into Gold" which is all about helping people identify their biggest assumptions and then turning them into breakthroughs. It's described in my new book, www.awakeatthewheel.info All the Best! Mitch Ditkoff
