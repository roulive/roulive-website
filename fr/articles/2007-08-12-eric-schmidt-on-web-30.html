Eric Schmidt on Web 3.0

12 août 2007
Innovation & santéInternet & tech

Short but interesting video. What does the CEO of Google answer "when asked "What is Web 3.0?" First, he makes a joke (because, as everyone else, he absolutely has no idea of what he's going to answer ;-)) Then, he says something general "Applications are small, fast, customized." Then something hype "The Data is in the cloud" And then, the real deal "Apps wile be distributed by virus. By social networks, by email. You won't go to the store to purchase them. Here is something interesting, I'll send it to you".

And that's interesting!
