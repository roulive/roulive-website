There will be ... a masterpiece

December 14, 2007
Cinema

This post was originally written in French.

Je vous parlais de la bande-annonce de There will be blood, le nouveau P.T. Anderson, il y a quelques temps.

Le film est aujourd'hui nominé au Golden Globes: - Best Motion Picture - Drama - Best Performance by an Actor in a Motion Picture - Drama ... mais pas de Best Director.

Mais le plus intéressant, ce sont les premiers avis.

Chez Newsweek: "[A] work [of] blistering intensity -- and filmmaking that can make your jaw drop.".

Chez ColeSmithey.com: "Paul Thomas Anderson has grown immensely as a writer/director since his last feature film, so much so that in a single film he has become America's most visionary and accomplished auteur."

Un grand film américain comme on en attend chaque année?
